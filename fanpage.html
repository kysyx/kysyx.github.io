Who He Is

Born April 7, 1954 in Hong Kong, Jackie Chan was enrolled as a child at the China Drama Academy, where he trained for a decade in acrobatics, martial arts, and Peking Opera performance alongside future collaborators Sammo Hung and Yuen Biao. That grueling training became the foundation for a career built on a simple, dangerous idea: perform the stunts for real, and let the camera run.

Across films like Drunken Master, Police Story, and Rush Hour, Chan pioneered a style that blended slapstick comedy with genuinely bone-crunching action, turning everyday objects and locations into elaborate fight choreography.
